Common jobs in Lightcliffe

From the big gardens off Wakefield Road to the streets around the Stray, the work is usually one of these.

Mature tree management

Beech, oak and horse chestnut inspected, dead-wooded and reduced on a sensible cycle - the way old trees stay safe and healthy.

Sectional dismantling

Trees that have outgrown their position taken down piece by piece on ropes, clear of conservatories, walls and planting below.

Boundary hedge work

Long established hedges trimmed, gapped-up or reduced, keeping the character of the street without the yearly battle.

Local knowledge that matters

Parts of Lightcliffe fall within a conservation area, and older trees can carry Tree Preservation Orders. The tree surgeons we work with check with Calderdale Council before any work starts and handle the application where one is needed - you never have to chase paperwork.
