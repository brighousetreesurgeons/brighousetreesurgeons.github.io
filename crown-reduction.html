Crown Work

Keep the tree, lose the problem.

A tree that has outgrown its space rarely needs to come down. Crown work reshapes it so it fits your garden again.

Get a free quote

What's involved

Crown reduction shortens the canopy all round, taking weight off overextended limbs. Crown thinning removes selected inner branches to let light and wind through without changing the outline. Crown lifting removes lower branches to clear roofs, driveways and paths. Done properly to British Standard BS3998, the tree stays healthy and looks natural.

Typical cost

From around £200 for a small ornamental tree to £800+ for a large mature tree. A proper reduction costs more than a hack job - and is worth it.

Good to know

Topping - cutting straight across the top - ruins trees and makes them more dangerous long-term. If someone suggests it, get a second opinion.
