Emergency Tree Work

Storm damage? Help fast.

A fallen or hanging tree is stressful and dangerous. Emergency enquiries are prioritised so the risk is dealt with first.

Report an urgent tree

What's involved

The first job is making the scene safe - removing hanging branches, clearing trees off buildings, and opening blocked drives and paths. Full removal or reduction follows once the immediate danger is gone. If a tree has damaged your property, the tree surgeon can document the work for your insurance claim.

Typical cost

Emergency call-outs cost more than planned work - expect from £300 for urgent make-safe work, always quoted before work starts.

Good to know

If a tree is touching power lines, stay well clear and call Northern Powergrid on 105 first. Trees on public roads are Calderdale Council's responsibility - we can advise either way.
