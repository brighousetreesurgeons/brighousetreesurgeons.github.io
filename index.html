Local knowledge matters

Brighouse has mature gardens, conservation areas and trees protected by Tree Preservation Orders. Work on a protected tree without Calderdale Council's consent can mean a heavy fine. A good tree surgeon checks for TPOs before touching anything - the ones we work with do this as standard, and handle the council application where one is needed.

Storm damage? Fallen or dangerous trees are prioritised. If a tree is threatening a building, a road or power lines, say so when you get in touch and it will be treated as urgent.
