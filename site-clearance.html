Common clearance jobs

Every plot is different, but most clearances in the area fall into a few shapes.

Before building work

Extensions, garages and garden rooms need clear ground. Trees and stumps removed along the build line, with root systems dealt with so nothing regrows under foundations.

Overgrown plots

Years of self-seeded saplings, brambles and scrub cut back and taken away - the plot you actually own, visible again.

Boundary lines

Vegetation cleared along fence and wall lines so boundaries can be repaired, replaced or surveyed properly.

Done properly

Clearance starts with the same checks as any tree work: protection status (see our TPO guide), nesting season, and what's staying versus going. Everything arisings is chipped and removed, stumps ground below ground level where needed, and the site left tidy for the next trade.
