What changes the price

Size and species

A small ornamental cherry is a different job from a 20m ash. Hardwood species take longer to cut and chip.

What's underneath

Greenhouses, sheds, conservatories, fences and power lines all mean slower, more careful rigging - and a higher price.

Access

Rear gardens down narrow passages cost more than front gardens with vehicle access. Gate width matters for stump grinders.

Waste

Keeping the timber and chip for yourself trims the price; full removal of everything costs more.

Beware very cheap quotes for climbing work. Proper tree surgeons carry public liability insurance and trained climbers - if a price seems too good to be true, ask to see insurance before agreeing.
