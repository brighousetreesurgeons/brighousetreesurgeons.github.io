The right season for every tree job.

Timing changes everything in tree work - how the tree responds, what it costs, and whether it's even legal that month. A season-by-season look for Brighouse gardens.

Autumn

Ideal for assessments and removals. Leaves dropping means the structure is visible, and booking now beats the winter storm rush. Fungi and decay are easiest to spot this season.

Winter

The classic pruning window: most trees are dormant, wounds close cleanly in spring, and bare crowns make crown reductions precise. Often the best-value season too.

Spring

Approach with care - sap is rising and nesting season begins. Light work only on most species, and every job starts with a nest check. Removing an active nest is against the law.

And summer?

Summer suits shaping, dead-wooding and dealing with storm damage. Some species, like cherries and plums, are actually best pruned in summer to avoid silver leaf disease. A good tree surgeon will tell you which rule your tree follows - the answer is rarely the same twice.

The one rule that always applies

Whatever the season, protected trees need consent first - see our guide to Tree Preservation Orders in Calderdale. And if a tree is storm-damaged or dangerous, don't wait for the "right" season: dangerous trees are dealt with as they are found.
