Tree Surgeon Rastrick

Tree work in Rastrick, done carefully.

Rastrick's gardens are full of mature trees - and many sit on sloped or terraced plots where access is half the job. We connect you with qualified, insured local tree surgeons who know the area and plan the work around your garden.

Common jobs in Rastrick

From the established gardens off Rastrick Common to the terraced streets towards the M62, the work is usually one of these.

Crown reductions on mature trees

Older sycamore, ash and beech that have outgrown their space - reduced carefully to keep light in the garden without butchering the tree.

Dismantling on sloped plots

Trees on Rastrick's hillsides and terraced gardens need rigging and lowering, not felling. Sections come down on ropes, clear of sheds, greenhouses and fences below.

Hedge lines and boundaries

Overgrown boundary hedges brought back to a manageable height and shape, with all arisings chipped and removed.

Local knowledge that matters

Parts of Rastrick sit within or near conservation considerations, and older trees can carry Tree Preservation Orders. The tree surgeons we work with check with Calderdale Council before any work starts and handle the application where one is needed - you never have to chase paperwork.
