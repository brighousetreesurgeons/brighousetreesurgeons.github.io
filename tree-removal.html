Tree Removal

Gone safely, section by section.

Whether a tree is dead, diseased, storm-damaged or simply in the wrong place, removal is planned around your property - not the other way round.

Get a free quote

What's involved

Most trees in Brighouse gardens are dismantled in sections rather than felled in one. The tree surgeon climbs or uses a platform, lowers each section on ropes, and keeps everything clear of roofs, greenhouses, fences and sheds. All waste is chipped and removed, and the timber can be left for your log burner if you want it.

Typical cost

From around £250 for a small tree to £1,500+ for a large dismantle over buildings. Size, access and what sits underneath the tree drive the price.

Good to know

Trees in conservation areas or with Tree Preservation Orders need Calderdale Council consent before removal. The tree surgeons we work with check this for you and handle the application.
